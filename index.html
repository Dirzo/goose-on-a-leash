Goose on a Leash

Act 1 · Willowmere + Reedmarsh part one · playtest

Controls

WASD
Move Rocco
Click
Fling Biscuit toward the cursor. He bites and holds whatever he hits.
Right-click / E
Yank. Light things come to you. Heavy things (mooring posts, Old Snapper) pull you to them, across the water too.
Space
Honk toward the cursor. Stuns, knocks back, rings the fishing bell.
Q
Let go. Calm goose: rampages, then waddles back. Furious goose: goes feral and comes for you once nearby enemies are gone.
F
Toss bread. Effect depends on the bread. Pick up loaves around the room.
C
Switch which bread F throws
P
Patch the backpack. Hits tear holes and the Moon Koi's water leaks out. 2 Reed Fiber per patch. Stand on the shoreline to refill.
B
Recipe book. Craft leashes and breads. Pauses the game.
Walk over leash
Grab the trailing leash of a feral or loose goose.
R
Restart the room from the door you came in by (satchel, leashes and pack carry over)
Doors
Walk off the edge of a room through a gap in the trees. Gates open when Biscuit honks the room's bell.
Test rooms
Add ?room=room-01 (gray-box) or ?room=reedmarsh-4 to the address to jump straight there.

The Goosekeeper's Recipe Book

Leashes keep you alive. Bread keeps him calm. Catalysts (✦) are rare and decide what kind of goose you're raising.
