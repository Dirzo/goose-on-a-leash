# Steam store copy and capsule brief

## Short description (300 characters max)

Rocco the raccoon fights a blood-moon curse with the only weapon he has: Biscuit, a furious goose on a fishing line.
Power-cast him, yank him, swing on him, and keep Bimbo the koi's backpack from leaking dry.

## About this game

**A fishing line. A goose. A very bad night.**

The blood moon has turned every critter in the valley red-eyed and mean. Rocco the raccoon heads out to break the curse
with his best friend Bimbo, a talking Moon Koi who rides in a backpack full of water. That water is your life. Every hit
splashes some out; every tear leaks. Find springs, patch the pack, and keep going.

**Biscuit is the weapon, and he is not on your side.** He's tied to the end of Rocco's fishing line. Hold to wind up,
release at the top of the swing for a perfect cast, and he bites hard. Yank him back across the room, swing across a
ravine on him, use him to pull Rocco to a post. Let him get too angry and he turns on you.

**Fishing is the other half.** Fight through the Tide Caves to Moonlit Cove and fish as long as you like: thirty kinds
across five depths, from sprat to the Moray Twins, each one a fight on the same power-cast and reel.

**Features**
* A hand-made pixel world in six regions, from the reed marshes to Castle Honkmore
* Eight bosses, each built around a different use of the goose
* A fishing game you can play for hours, with a journal to fill
* The Infinite Basement: endless floors, a growing critter mix, a leaderboard
* Story, Normal and Brutal; full gamepad support; rebindable keys; shake, flash and colour-safe options

## Capsule brief (for the artist)

Sizes: header 920 × 430, small 462 × 174, main 1232 × 706, vertical 748 × 896, library hero 3840 × 1240, library logo.

* **Focal point:** Biscuit the goose mid-flight on a taut fishing line, wings spread, beak open, coming straight at the viewer.
* **Behind him:** Rocco the raccoon braced, rod bent, the backpack on his back with Bimbo (an orange-and-white koi, small crown of fins) peering out of the water.
* **Sky:** a huge red blood moon; the line crosses it. Pale blue night palette elsewhere so the red reads.
* **Logo:** "Goose on a Leash" in the chunky display face from the game, cream with a dark outline; the "o" in Goose can be the moon.
* **Mood:** funny first, a bit dangerous. Pixel art at 3× or painted-over pixel art; no 3D.
* **Small capsule:** logo and Biscuit's head only. It has to read at 120 px wide.
* Avoid: text other than the logo, real brands, any other game's characters.
