Goose on a Leash

M1 build · Phaser 4 · Room 01

Controls

WASD
Move the janitor
Click
Fling the goose toward the cursor. It bites and holds whatever it hits.
Right-click / E
Yank. Light things come to you. Heavy things (posts, knights) pull you to them, over pits too.
Space
Honk toward the cursor. Stuns, knocks back, rings bells.
Q
Let go. Calm goose: rampages, then waddles back. Furious goose: goes feral and comes for you once nearby enemies are gone.
F
Toss bread. Effect depends on the bread. Pick up loaves around the room.
C
Switch which bread F throws
B
Recipe book. Craft leashes and breads. Pauses the game.
Walk over leash
Grab the trailing leash of a feral or loose goose.
R
Reset the room (your satchel and leashes carry over)

Tuning bench

Sliders set the base. Your equipped leash adds its bonuses on top, shown on the HUD under the strain bar.

The Goosekeeper's Recipe Book

Leashes keep you alive. Bread keeps him calm. Catalysts (✦) are rare and decide what kind of goose you're raising.
