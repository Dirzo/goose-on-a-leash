# Community: Discord, beta and feedback

## Discord server outline

* **#welcome** (read only): what the game is, the rules, how to get the beta role.
* **#announcements** (read only): builds and dates.
* **#general**
* **#screenshots-and-clips**
* **#fishing-brag**: catches, journal pages, biggest fish (the cove is the most shareable part).
* **#basement-leaderboard**: floors reached; pin a weekly top five.
* **#bug-reports**: one thread per bug. Pinned template below.
* **#feedback**: what felt good, what felt unfair.
* **beta** category (role-gated): **#beta-builds**, **#beta-feedback**.

Roles: Beta Tester, Angler (posted a legendary catch), Basement Dweller (floor 10+). Use a simple reaction-role bot.

### Bug report template

```
What happened:
What you expected:
Room (top-left of the bench, or the room name on screen):
Difficulty:
Browser / OS / input (mouse, gamepad):
Playtest stats (bench → Playtest stats, paste):
```

## Closed beta plan

1. **Size:** 20 to 40 players. Mix of people who like action games and people who only want the fishing.
2. **Build:** the Cloudflare preview URL or the itch.io page set to "Restricted" with a password.
3. **Two weeks.** Week one: play from the start on Normal. Week two: free play, Basement runs, Brutal.
4. **Ask for:** the Playtest stats paste (it shows where they fainted and where they stopped), and three answers:
   * Where did you stop, and why?
   * Which fight felt unfair?
   * How long did you stay at Moonlit Cove?
5. **Read the stats first.** The rooms at the top of "most faints" and "sessions ended in" are the balance work (see `docs/BALANCE.md`).
