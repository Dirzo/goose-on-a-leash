## Launch trailer, 90 seconds

1. **Cold open (0:00 to 0:10).** The prologue: Rocco at the pond, Biscuit grabbed, the moon turns red. Bimbo: "Rocco. ROCCO. The moon is doing the thing."
2. **The verb (0:10 to 0:25).** Teach the power cast in three shots: a weak cast falling short, a full cast, a perfect cast with the flash. Then yank, then the oar parry sending an acorn back.
3. **The world (0:25 to 0:45).** One establishing shot per region, 3 seconds each, each with its music sting: Reedmarsh, Hollowbark, Driftwood Wharf, Tide Caves, Castle Honkmore.
4. **The stakes (0:45 to 0:55).** The backpack: a hit tears it, water leaks, patch it from the backpack screen. Cracked glasses and a stumble.
5. **The fishing (0:55 to 1:08).** Moonlit Cove at night, three catches getting bigger, the journal page filling, the Moray Twins.
6. **The bosses (1:08 to 1:22).** Two seconds each: Old Snapper, the Heron Ferryman, Gullbeard, the Pelican Chef, the Black Swan, the Swan Queen. End on the Blood Shark's fin.
7. **The basement (1:22 to 1:27).** A crowded high floor; the leaderboard.
8. **Card (1:27 to 1:30).** Logo, platforms, "a noobscrub game".
