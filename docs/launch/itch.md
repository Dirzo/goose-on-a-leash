## Short description (tagline)

A raccoon, a koi in a backpack, and a furious goose on a fishing line against a blood moon.

## Page copy

> Rocco the raccoon is a fisherman. His best friend, Bimbo the Moon Koi, rides in a backpack full of water, and that
> water is your life. His only weapon is Biscuit, a goose tied to the end of his fishing line who hates everything,
> Rocco included.
>
> Hold to wind up a cast, release at the top of the swing, and Biscuit flies. Land a perfect cast and he bites twice
> as hard. Yank him back, swing across ravines on him, honk critters out of their trance, and keep the backpack
> patched, because every hit splashes Bimbo's water and every tear leaks.
>
> * Six regions: Reedmarsh, Hollowbark Woods, Driftwood Wharf, the Tide Caves, Castle Honkmore and the Blood Moat
> * Eight bosses, from Old Snapper to the Swan Queen and the Blood Shark
> * Moonlit Cove: thirty kinds of fish to catch, as long as you like
> * The Infinite Basement: endless floors and a leaderboard
> * Story, Normal and Brutal difficulty; rebindable keys, gamepad support, shake and flash toggles
>
> Your progress saves in the browser. Best with a mouse, or a gamepad.

## Controls block

WASD move · hold left mouse to wind up, release to cast · right mouse yank (or raise the oar) · Space honk ·
Shift roll · F throw bread · C swap bread · P patch the pack · I backpack · B recipe book · J fish journal · R restart room.
All rebindable under **Settings & keys**.
