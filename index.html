Goose on a Leash

Act 1 · Willowmere · Reedmarsh · Hollowbark Woods · Driftwood Wharf

Rooms

Jumping keeps your satchel. Rooms past The Braided Line give you the Braided Line if you don't have it yet. Reset game wipes the save and starts again at Willowmere.

Controls

WASD
Move Rocco
Hold click, release
Cast Biscuit toward the cursor. Hold to wind up: the meter swings up and back, and the power sets how far he goes. Let go in the gold at the top for a perfect cast (a harder, stunning bite). He bites and holds whatever he hits; hold again while he bites to let line out. Q cuts the line once he's angry enough.
Right-click / E
Yank. Light things come to you. Heavy things (mooring posts, Old Snapper) pull you to them, across the water too.
Space
Honk toward the cursor. Stuns, knocks back, rings the fishing bell.
Shift
Roll. A quick tumble you can't be hit in. The leash still holds: latched to a post it catches you, and Biscuit gets hauled along behind. The pack sloshes a little.
G / hold right mouse
Guard (once the Ferryman gives you his oar). Blocks blows from the front while you walk slowly. Raise it just as an acorn or rock lands to parry: it flies back where you aim. Planted, it also braces you against Biscuit's pull. (Right mouse no longer yanks once you have it: use E.)
Q
Let go. Calm goose: rampages, then waddles back. Furious goose: goes feral and comes for you once nearby enemies are gone.
F
Toss bread. Effect depends on the bread. Pick up loaves around the room.
C
Switch which bread F throws
Bimbo
Bimbo the Moon Koi's water is your life. Hits splash water out of the backpack or tear it, and every tear makes it leak faster. Stand on a shoreline to refill. If the water runs out, Bimbo faints and you restart the room.
P
Patch a tear. Costs one needle use (sewing kits hold three) and one twine. Out of needle? Race to the next sewing kit.
I / Tab
Backpack: Bimbo, the patches you've sewn, the leak rate and your supplies. Pauses the game; press P there to patch.
B
Recipe book. Craft leashes and breads, and see Biscuit's traits. Pauses the game.
Shrine
Biscuit's shrine in Willowmere: spend Golden Feathers (from bosses, and a few hidden spots) on traits. Every two traits add one Goose Power.
M
Sound on / off
Walk over leash
Grab the trailing leash of a feral or loose goose.
R
Restart the room from the door you came in by (satchel, leashes and pack carry over)
Doors
Walk off the edge of a room through a gap in the trees. Gates open when Biscuit honks the room's bell.
Test rooms
Add ?room=room-01 (gray-box) or ?room=reedmarsh-4 to the address to jump straight there.
